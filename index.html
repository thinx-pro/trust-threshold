How much can your model decide on its own? Paste its confidences and whether each answer was right. You get the threshold that meets your target accuracy, the share of traffic that clears it, and a split-half check of whether that number holds up.

Threshold = the lowest confidence where items at or above it are right at least the target share of the time. The split-half check picks the threshold on a random half and measures it on the other half. · Source & docs · MIT
